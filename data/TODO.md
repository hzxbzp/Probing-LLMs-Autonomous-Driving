# Data — To Be Released

The following datasets will be made publicly available upon paper acceptance:

- **Scenario configurations:** Contextual factor combinations for all four driving scenarios (1,500 cases each)
- **LLM responses:** Raw responses from GPT-4o, DeepSeek-V3, and Llama 3.1-405B across three languages
- **Encoded datasets:** Structured datasets with behavioral option codes and factor encodings
- **Thematic analysis results:** NLI-based theme extraction outputs
- **OLM estimation data:** Final datasets used for Ordered Logit Model estimation
